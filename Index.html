90-day execution thesis

Days 1–30: Build and validate the account universe, research the highest-scoring districts, test role-based outreach.

Days 31–60: Double down on segments generating meetings, qualify buying processes, and build multi-threaded opportunities.

Days 61–90: Rebalance effort using meetings, qualified pipeline, cycle signals and actual conversion rates.

Interview-ready talk track

“I wouldn't treat 15,000 districts as 15,000 equal opportunities. I'd start with a clear enrollment-based segmentation, layer in need, readiness and access, and work a manageable active set. Enterprise gives me upside, while mid-sized and smaller districts create faster feedback and potential early wins. I'd revisit the mix every 30 days based on what the market tells me.”

Why these counts?

A research universe allows broad market coverage without pretending every district is in an active sales motion. The smaller active list creates room for meaningful multi-threaded outreach, qualification, follow-up and meetings. Adjust both to fit actual SDR support, territory ownership, average sales cycle and activity capacity.
